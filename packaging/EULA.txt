2. WHAT THE SOFTWARE DOES, AND DOES NOT DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PreflightQC inspects video files and reports whether their technical properties
meet a chosen delivery specification. It does not modify, encode, transcode,
repair or upload your files, and it makes no network connections.

PreflightQC reports what the file's metadata says. Acceptance by any platform is
that platform's decision, and platforms change their requirements without
notice. A PASS result is not a guarantee of acceptance, and the Software is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by any platform whose specifications
