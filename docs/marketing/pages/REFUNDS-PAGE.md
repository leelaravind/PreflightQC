# PreflightQC — Refund Policy

PreflightQC is a **one-time purchase of £19.99 (GBP)**. There is no subscription and
no automatic renewal — nothing recurs, so there is nothing to cancel.

**Refunds.** If PreflightQC is not right for you, request a refund within **7
calendar days** of purchase. Eligible refunds normally receive the **full purchase
price**. Refunds are processed through the merchant of record or platform you bought
from, using their refund mechanism. Nothing in this policy limits your statutory
rights, and where the platform's own refund rules give you more, those apply.

## How to request

1. Use the refund option in your purchase receipt or the storefront's order page
   (Lemon Squeezy or Gumroad, depending on where you bought).
2. If you cannot find it, contact {{SUPPORT_CONTACT}} with your order reference and
   we will point you to the right place.

## The details, plainly

- The 7 days are calendar days, counted from the purchase timestamp on your receipt.
- A refund is for the full amount paid. There are no deductions, no percentage fees,
  and no prorating — a refund is not reduced by how long you had the software or how
  much you used it.
- Refunds are issued by the merchant of record to your original payment method, on
  their timeline.
- Statutory consumer rights in your country are unaffected and take precedence
  wherever they differ from this policy, as do any mandatory refund rules of the
  platform you purchased through.
